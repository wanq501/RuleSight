These five prompts are the per-category prompts published with UI-Lens (Xiang et al., CVPR 2026, supplementary material, Appendix C), one per task: TO text overflow, CC cropped content, CO container overlap, UC undisplayed content, AE abnormal text ellipsis. They are reproduced for the UI-Lens prompting baseline with their role definition, detection guidelines and reasoning steps; run_api.py replaces their output format with the JSON format of the system prompt. They lie outside rulesight/prompts, so the prompt version of the stored answers is unchanged.
